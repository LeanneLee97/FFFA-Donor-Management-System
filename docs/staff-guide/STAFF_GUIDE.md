# FFFA Donor Management: Staff Guide

*Version 1 · 28 October 2026 · for FFFA staff*

This system keeps every donor relationship in one place. Every day it tells you **who to follow up with and why**, and it helps you write a warm, personal message in a minute instead of twenty. Nothing is ever sent without you reading and approving it.

> **Before it goes live** the system runs on a laptop with practice donors (made-up people, not real data). The separate **Handover Guide** explains how to run it and put it online. Once it's live you open it from a web link with your own login, and everything below works the same way.

---

## 1. Your daily routine (10–15 minutes)

1. Open **Follow-ups**. The list on the left is today's work, with the most urgent first.
2. Click a name. The right side shows who they are, what they've given, your notes and past messages.
3. Click **Generate draft**, read it, and change anything that doesn't sound like you.
4. Click **Approve**, then **Copy** or **Open in email**, and send it from your own email or WhatsApp.
5. Come back and click **Mark as sent**. The message is saved on the donor's profile and the item leaves your list.

## 2. What each follow-up means

| Label | Why it appears | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Thank-you due** | A gift (money or goods) from 3+ days ago hasn't been thanked | Send a thank-you. Draft it here. |
| **Failed payment** | A monthly gift on Giving.sg / GIVE.asia didn't go through (in the last 7 days) | Send a gentle note asking them to update their card. No pressure. |
| **Key date** | A sponsorship renewal, grant report or grant renewal is due within 30 days | Get in touch about the renewal or report. |
| **Going cold** | No contact for a while (how long depends on how much they give) | Quick check-in with no ask. |
| **Lapsed** | No gift for 45 days (monthly donors) or 6 months (one-off donors) | A light "we miss you" note. |
| **Anniversary** | Their first gift was a year ago (or more) this week | Celebrate how long they've been with us. |
| **Upgrade ask** | Same monthly amount for 12+ months | Thank them first, then one gentle invitation to give a little more. |

These timings are starting points, and an admin can change them.

## 3. Writing a message

![A draft ready to edit and approve](2-draft.png)

- **Generate draft** writes a message using only what's on the donor's profile: their gifts, programme interest, notes and key dates. It never sees email addresses, phone numbers or ID numbers, and any numbers typed into notes are removed first.
- **Always read it before approving.** Check names, amounts and dates. Change the tone so it sounds like FFFA. You are the sender, not the system.
- **Regenerate** gives you a fresh version. **Discard** throws the draft away.
- **Approve** locks in your final text. Then send it yourself (**Copy** or **Open in email**) and click **Mark as sent**, choosing how you sent it: email, WhatsApp, call, meeting or other.
- Impact lines use FFFA's figure of **S$10 = 1 meal for one elderly person**. The system does the maths, so the numbers are always right.
- The label "Template" on a draft means it came from a standard wording instead of AI. You edit and send it the same way.

---

## 4. Major donors: always personal

![Major donors get a personal touch instead of a draft](3-major.png)

A donor counts as **major** if they gave **S$5,000+ in the last 12 months**, have been with FFFA **3+ years**, or have been **tagged by staff**. For them, AI drafting is switched off. Reach out personally (call, meet or write yourself), then log the conversation on their profile so the relationship stays with FFFA and not with one person.

## 5. The dashboard

![Dashboard: totals only, no personal details](4-dashboard.png)

The **Dashboard** tab gives the big picture: number of donors, giving in the last 12 months, monthly donors, major donors, follow-ups waiting, messages sent, giving by month and by campaign, and donors by tier. It shows totals only, so it's safe to put on screen in a meeting.

*Tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold) are for internal planning only. Never mention them to donors.*

---

## 6. Bringing in Giving.sg and GIVE.asia donations

On the **Import** screen, choose the platform and upload the donations export from Giving.sg or GIVE.asia as a CSV file. The system then:

- shows you a preview first, and nothing is saved until you confirm
- **never double-counts**: importing the same file twice adds nothing
- recognises the **same person on both platforms** by email or mobile, so their profile shows all their giving together
- **flags rows it isn't sure about** (for example, same name but a different email) for you to check, instead of guessing
- adds thank-you follow-ups for new gifts straight away

## 7. House rules

- **Read every message before it goes out.** The draft is a starting point, never the final word.
- **Never type NRIC, FIN, UEN or bank details** into notes or anywhere else in the system.
- **Log what matters in Notes**, like "prefers WhatsApp" or "mum used RTE meals". Better notes mean better messages, and the history stays with FFFA if someone leaves.
- **Practice data only** until the system is live with logins. Don't enter real donors into a laptop copy.
- If a draft says something wrong or odd, **don't send it**. Fix it, and tell whoever looks after the system so the wording can be improved.

---

## 8. Getting your data ready

- Download the last 12 months of donations from **Giving.sg** and **GIVE.asia** as CSV, ready to import.
- List your **major donors** and anyone who should always get a personal touch, so they can be tagged.
- List upcoming **key dates**: sponsorship renewals, grant reports, grant renewals.
- Agree the **sign-off** used at the end of messages (default: "Warm regards, The Free Food For All team").
- Decide **who gets a login** and who is an admin (admins can change settings and remove donor records for PDPA requests).

**Questions or problems?** Contact whoever looks after the system for FFFA. The Handover Guide explains how to make changes.
